For every report, investigate whether its numerical values represent:

- Monthly generation.
- Cumulative year-to-date generation.
- Financial-year generation.
- Calendar-year generation.
- Daily generation.

Do not add station totals to state totals or state totals to regional totals.

## D5. Data quality checks

Perform these checks wherever the data structure supports them.

### Completeness
- Missing values.
- Missing years.
- Missing months.
- Missing states.
- Missing energy sources.

### Consistency
- Different state spellings.
- Different energy source labels.
- Inconsistent units.
- Duplicate records.
- Inconsistent date formats.

### Temporal integrity
- Monthly versus cumulative data.
- Financial-year versus calendar-year reporting.
- Missing reporting periods.
- Duplicate reporting periods.

### Numerical integrity
- Negative generation values.
- Unexpected zero values.
- Extreme outliers.
- Sudden unexplained changes.
- Invalid numeric values.

### Reconciliation
- State totals versus national totals.
- Regional totals versus national totals.
- Station totals versus state totals.
- Source-wise generation versus overall generation.

Only perform reconciliation where the geographical coverage, reporting period, source classification and units match.

Do not force totals to match by modifying source data.
