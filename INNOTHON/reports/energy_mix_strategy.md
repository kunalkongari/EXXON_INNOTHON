# Energy-Mix Strategy (conventional sources)

_Generated by `src/strategy.py` on 2026-11-15 from Phase 2-3 outputs._

> **Scope.** Domestic **conventional** generation monitored by CEA (coal, lignite, gas, diesel, nuclear, large hydro). Solar, wind, small hydro and biomass generation are **not quantified** (no authorised actual-generation data), so totals are **not** India's total electricity production or requirement. Generation location is not consumption. Bhutan import is separate.

**Two separate analyses.** (1) A *trend-based reference*, which is a statistical extrapolation of shares with its artefacts flagged. (2) *Policy scenarios*: **recommendations, not forecasts**. Both allocate the approved domestic conventional totals. In every case the sources sum exactly to that total, with coal/lignite as the balancing item. Lignite is separated from coal only nationally (FY2025-26 lignite share of coal+lignite = 2.37%).
**Solar, wind, small hydro and biomass are not quantified.**

- **LIGNITE**: Steady decline 2.65% -> 1.98% (consistent direction all 7 FYs); damped trend is plausible but rests on 7 points - QUALIFIED.

## 3. Policy scenarios (recommendations)
| policy scenario | rule | justification (data) |
|---|---|---|
| P0_status_quo_FY2025_26 | Hold the latest observed mix (FY2025-26). | Latest complete year; no change in relative roles of sources. |
| P1_historical_average | Mean shares FY2019-20..FY2025-26. | Smooths hydrology (wet/dry years) and gas-price episodes across 7 observed years. |
| P2_demonstrated_max_share | Each non-coal source at its highest observed FY share (hydro, nuclear, gas); lignite and diesel at FY2025-26 share; coal = balance. | Every share has already been achieved by the existing system within FY2019-20..FY2025-26; lowest coal dependence demonstrated in the data. |
| P3_no_decline_floor | Each non-coal source at least its highest observed annual generation (TWh) - never below demonstrated output; otherwise FY2025-26 share; coal/lignite = balance. | Protects demonstrated hydro, nuclear and gas output from being crowded out; effect largest in the low scenario. |

Not used: any source percentage without an observed basis; any capacity, consumption or renewable input; new-build assumptions (installed capacity is prohibited as a basis).

## 6. Recommendation
- **Reference case for planning:** T3 x central scenario.
- **Preferred policy direction: P2 (demonstrated maximum shares).** It lowers coal dependence to the lowest level already achieved by the existing system, without assuming any untested capability.
- **Use P3 (no-decline floor) as a safeguard** in the low scenario, so demonstrated hydro, nuclear and gas output is not crowded out.
- Regional allocations of every mix (region x source, IPF-balanced to both regional and national totals) are in `outputs/strategy/regional_source_mix_2047.csv`.
